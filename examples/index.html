The soundfield

Twelve shapes, one rule-set. Position sets the stereo placement, size sets pitch and weight, corner radius morphs the wave from square through sawtooth and triangle to sine — the Kiki/Bouba effect, made executable.

How it hears your page

Ten mappings, each backed by cross-modal perception research — see the Mapping Canon for formulas and the research notes for citations.

↔Position → stereo stage

An element's x/y becomes its place in a 3D sound field. Left button, left sound. HRTF spatialization.

◻→◯Roundness → waveform

Border radius morphs the oscillator: sharp corners bite (square), pills breathe (sine). Kiki/Bouba, executable.

⤢Size → pitch & weight

Big elements speak low and loud; small ones chirp high and quiet — the physics of resonance, borrowed.

▭Elongation → duration

A square ticks; a long bar sweeps. Shape length maps to note length.

≣DOM depth → distance

Deeply nested elements sound farther away and more muffled — the tree becomes acoustic depth.

♫Siblings → melody

Children of one parent climb the same scale. A nav is a run; a list is an arpeggio.

⌂Viewport → the room

Page width sets the reverb. A phone is a dry booth; a wide desktop is a hall.

#Domain → key

The hostname hashes to a musical key and scale — every site gets a deterministic sonic identity.

⇪Shadow → air

Box-shadow blur lifts an element into the reverb: elevated UI floats in the room.

⌨Typing → filling up

Keystrokes tick upward as a field fills. Toggles answer with rising or falling fifths.
